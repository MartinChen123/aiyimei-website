About Us

About AIYIMEI

AIYIMEI Medical Technology specializes in R&D, manufacturing, and sales of injectable hyaluronic acid (HA) dermal fillers, headquartered in Guangzhou, China.

Our GMP cleanroom and ISO 13485 system cover lip augmentation, cheek lifting, wrinkle correction, and facial contouring — wholesale and OEM/ODM for Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and beyond.

Expertise

Our Areas of Expertise

Core technical strengths that set us apart in the global aesthetics market

01

Cross-Linking Technology

Proprietary BDDE cross-linking with precise G-prime, swelling ratio, and degradation kinetics — optimized per injection plane.

02

HA Purification

Multi-stage purification. Endotoxin below Pharmacopoeia limits. HA purity ≥99.5% for maximum biocompatibility.

03

Rheology Control

Precise viscoelastic control (G', G'', complex modulus) matched to tissue mechanics at each injection depth.

04

GMP-Compliant Manufacturing

Class 10,000 cleanroom, continuous monitoring, validated sterilization, and full batch traceability.

05

OEM / ODM Services

From formula customization to packaging design and regulatory support — full private label solutions.

06

Export Compliance Support

Registration assistance for target markets: stability data, biocompatibility reports, and full technical documentation.
