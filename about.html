Our Story

Expanded product line to 30+ SKUs covering all facial application zones
2023

2025
New state-of-the-art facility with expanded production capacity; serving 20+ countries

Our Professional Fields

Core competencies that differentiate us in the global aesthetics market

🔬

Cross-Linking Technology

Advanced BDDE cross-linking process with precise control over gel hardness (G-prime), swelling ratio, and degradation kinetics for optimal clinical performance.

🔬

HA Purification

Multi-stage purification process ensures endotoxin levels well below Pharmacopoeia limits, with purity exceeding 99.5% for maximum biocompatibility.

🔬

Rheology Control

Precise control over viscoelastic properties — elasticity (G'), viscosity (G''), and complex modulus — tailored for each injection plane and clinical indication.
